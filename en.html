About me

My name is Roman and I've been doing photography and video since 2020. I study photography and I love creating where things are happening: at events, balls, sports matches and portrait shoots. I also gain experience as a long-time volunteer at the Ji.hlava International Documentary Film Festival (Ji.hlava) and I've worked on many projects for my school and for the Vysočina Region. Outside of shooting, I love to travel, preferably low cost, and I document everything on my second Instagram account @laromcino.

How I work & specialties

I work reliably, with a focus on atmosphere, emotion and the quality of the final shot. I always try to capture moments so they feel natural and have their own dynamics. I generally don't deliver work I'm not happy with myself.
